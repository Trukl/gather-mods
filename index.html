Features

Everything Gather doesn't do. Yet.

Each module adds a section to the Mods panel. Here is what they look like in a real space.

Fun

Make the map move.

The animation studio turns any objects into a show: pick them on the map, click points to draw a path, and tune every step.

  • Shapes: lines, waves, arcs, orbits, or your own x(t) and y(t) formulas.
  • Easings: smooth, overshoot, bounce, elastic, or your own f(t); with pauses and turns.
  • As one block or each on its own, with a stagger. Loop, ping-pong or play once.
  • In sync for everyone on the map who has the module. And Flee mode keeps you away from a chosen player.
Custom objects

Put your own images on the map.

Gather V2 can't add custom objects. This module can: any image, anywhere, seen by everyone who has it.

  • Upload or paste a URL, then drop it where you click. Set its size in tiles, layer, opacity and collision.
  • Restyle a real object: your image over it, keeping its collision and seats.
  • Animated GIFs play on the map, in sync for everyone.
  • Bring to front or send back, place copies like a Gather object, animate them with Fun.
Build

Build what Gather won't let you.

The build tool, without the guardrails.

  • Free placement: nothing snaps to tiles (hold ⌘ to snap).
  • Place on…: put objects on any other object, even ones Gather refuses.
  • Show linked objects: see what sits on what, outlined on the map.
  • Bulk delete: drag a box, delete everything inside at once.
Desks & Constructions

Redecorate in one click.

Save what you built once, bring it back anytime.

  • Desk presets: swap your whole desk decoration. Only the objects that differ change, and an automatic backup keeps the previous one.
  • Constructions: save a group of objects, then place it anywhere: a ghost follows your pointer.
  • Placed objects: a journal of everything the mod placed, to delete it again.
Community hub

Borrow from everyone.

Browse the outfits, desks and constructions other people shared, preview them on hover, add them in one click. Publish yours just as easily.

  • Search by name, author or content.
  • Signed in with your Gather account: what you publish stays yours to delete.
  • Share presets also exports and imports them as JSON.
Seasonal themes

Your office, in season.

Halloween, Christmas and Easter looks that turn on by themselves: the interface takes the season's colors, every catalog object gets decorated, and bats, snow or petals drift over the map. Only on your screen.

  • Halloween: Oct 1 to Nov 15.
  • Christmas: Dec 1 to Jan 15.
  • Easter: three weeks before Easter Sunday to two weeks after.
Auto away

Step away, your status follows.

Away or Busy after a few minutes without keyboard or mouse, back as soon as you return. Never during a conversation or meeting.

Core

Spot the other modders.

Players running the mod get a halo under their avatar and a puzzle piece after their name. You can hide yours.

The panel

Feels like Gather.

Light or dark, the panel and its windows follow Gather's look. Fold a section and it still tells you what's running.

Install

Up and running in two minutes.

The macOS app keeps your modules updated. The browser way works anywhere, no install needed.

  1. Check the requirements

    The Gather desktop app in /Applications/GatherV2.app, and Node.js 22.9 or later.

  2. Build “Gather Mods.app”

    In a terminal:

    $ git clone https://github.com/Trukl/gather-mod-menu.git
$ cd gather-mod-menu
$ npm ci --omit=dev
$ node scripts/build-launcher-app.mjs

    This creates ~/Applications/Gather Mods.app.

  3. Open Gather through it

    From now on, open Gather Mods.app instead of GatherV2: it starts Gather with the mod. The Mods panel shows up in the sidebar; pick your modules in ⚙ Settings.

Modules update from inside Gather. Every 15 minutes the app checks for new versions; you see the changelog and the code diff, then install. Automatic updates are off by default.

Updating the app itself (new launcher, new icon):

git pull && npm ci --omit=dev && node scripts/build-launcher-app.mjs

Opened from the Dock or at login, Gather runs without the mod. The app drives Gather through a debug port: it only listens on your computer, but any local program can use it while Gather runs this way.

  1. Open Gather in your browser

    Go to app.v2.gather.town and join your space.

  2. Copy the mod

    All modules in one file, straight from the repo.

    View the code

  3. Paste it in the console

    Open the DevTools console (⌥ ⌘ J on Mac, Ctrl Shift J elsewhere), paste, press Enter. Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first.

  4. Pick your modules

    The Mods panel appears in the sidebar. Choose your modules in ⚙ Settings.

No automatic updates in this mode. The mod lives until you reload the tab: paste the latest script again to update.

Auto away needs the desktop app: a browser tab can't tell whether you are using your computer.

Only paste code you trust in a console. This button copies gather-mod-menu.js from the main branch of the public repo, nothing else.

Safety

Signed. Checked. Reviewed.

Code that runs in your Gather should be code you can trust. Every update goes through the same signed pipeline, and the app refuses anything that doesn't match.

  1. Merged on mainEvery change lands through a pull request, in public.
  2. Release workflowGitHub Actions builds the module catalog.
  3. Sigstore signatureSigned for that workflow, logged in the public Rekor log.
  4. Launcher checksSignature, workflow identity, code hashes, no downgrade.
  5. Runs in GatherOnly after you review it, unless you turn on auto-update.

See the diff, then install

“Review & install” shows each module's changelog next to the exact code diff. Compare it with the public commit on GitHub.

Only what you install runs

Modules you don't install never reach Gather. Uninstalling one keeps its data, in case you change your mind.

Your account id, nothing more

The community hub verifies your Gather sign-in and keeps only your account id: never your token, never your email.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this made by Gather?

No. Gather Mod Menu is an unofficial, open-source project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Gather. It runs on your side, in your Gather app or tab.

Who sees my animations and custom images?

Everyone on the map who has the same module installed: they are stored on the mod's community hub and drawn by each mod. Nothing is sent to Gather's server, so people without the mod see the map as usual.

And what I build with Build, Desks or Constructions?

Those are real map edits, made through Gather like its own build tool: everyone sees them. The Placed objects journal lists everything the mod placed, so you can delete it again.

Does it work on Windows or Linux?

The desktop app (“Gather Mods.app”) is macOS only. On any other system, use the browser way: paste the script into the console of a Gather tab.

How do updates work?

With the macOS app, the mod checks for new module versions every 15 minutes. A dot in the panel tells you an update is ready; “Review & install” shows what changed and installs it live, no restart. You can also turn on automatic updates.

When an update is required (an older version no longer works), the module stays off and a window offers to update it.

Will a space admin's new map version break my stuff?

No. Animations, placed objects, desk objects, saved locations and custom images all survive a new version of the map, even when everything moved.

How do I remove it?

Uninstall single modules from ⚙ Settings. To run Gather without the mod, open it normally instead of through “Gather Mods.app” (or reload the tab in the browser).
